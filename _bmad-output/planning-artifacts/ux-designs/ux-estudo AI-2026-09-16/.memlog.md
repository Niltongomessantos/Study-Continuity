- (decision) MVP UX is initially for a solo user. Primary form factor is desktop/laptop; responsive/mobile support is deferred. Home prioritizes the next scheduled Session, with past and upcoming Sessions below at lower visual emphasis. Opening the next Session shows Start and Stop controls. Home also shows completed lessons for the current week and missed lessons. When no Session is scheduled, the primary area states that clearly and the day agenda below shows past and upcoming Sessions. Future week view expansion is out of current MVP scope. Initial visual direction should remain generic and be refined later.
- (decision) Stopping a Managed Lesson or Free Block applies the 50% planned-duration rule: Completed at or above 50%, Lost below 50%. Weekly history shows counters only for completed and missed lessons. Past and upcoming agenda Sessions are consult-only for now; editing and rescheduling from the agenda are deferred.
- (event) spines finalized after structure and prose review; DESIGN.md tokens added, EXPERIENCE.md clarified, MVP scope preserved
